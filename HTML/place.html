My Favorite Place - Chennai

About Chennai

Welcome to my webpage about Chennai!

Chennai is a beautiful city located on the Coromandel Coast of India. It is known for its beaches, temples, culture, and food.

Chennai is also called the Gateway of South India. The city has a rich history and is famous for its traditional music and dance.

Famous Places in Chennai

  • Marina Beach
  • Kapaleeshwarar Temple
  • Fort St. George
  • Government Museum
  • Valluvar Kottam

Things to Do

  1. Visit Marina Beach
  2. Explore historical places
  3. Try traditional South Indian food
  4. Visit temples and museums

Interesting Facts

Chennai was formerly known as Madras. The city is famous for Marina Beach.
